# Privacy Policy

**Learning Tracker** is a fully offline, single-file Progressive Web App. This policy explains how your data is handled.

## Data Storage

All data you enter — subjects, weeks, learning outcomes, confidence ratings, notes, mistakes, exam dates, and study streaks — is stored exclusively in your browser's `localStorage` on your own device. No data is ever transmitted to any server.

## No Data Collection

- No analytics or tracking of any kind
- No cookies
- No accounts or sign-in required
- No third-party scripts or services
- No advertising

## No Internet Required

After the first load, the app works completely offline via a service worker cache. Your data never leaves your device under any circumstances.

## Data Export & Deletion

- You can export all your data at any time using the **Save JSON** button
- You can delete all app data by clearing your browser's site data for this page (`Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data`)
- Uninstalling the PWA also removes all locally stored data

## Changes

This app has no backend, so there is nothing to breach. If this policy ever changes it will be updated in this file.
